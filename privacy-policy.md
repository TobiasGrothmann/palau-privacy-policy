# Privacy Policy

Palau takes your privacy seriously. To better protect your privacy I provide this privacy policy notice explaining the way your personal information is collected and used.

## Collection of Routine Information

This app does not track or collect any information about its users. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your lists are stored only on your device, unless you choose to share a list as described in the Shared Lists section below.

## Shared Lists

If you choose to share a list, or join a list someone shared with you, that list and its entries are synced through Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so that everyone who shares it sees the same content. This data is stored on Google's servers and is used solely for the purpose of syncing the shared list. Lists you don't share never leave your device.

- To sync, the app signs in to Firebase anonymously. No name, email address or other personal information is needed or collected.
- A shared list can be read and edited by anyone who has its share key. Only give the key to people you want to share the list with.
- Entries deleted from a shared list are removed from the servers about a year after deletion.
- Leaving a shared list removes it from your device. The other people in the list keep their copy.

## Cookies

This app does not use any cookies.

## Security

The security of your personal information is important to me, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While I strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, I cannot guarantee its absolute security.
